# Anti-AI-Slop Framework

> Enforces production-grade quality, authentic brand identity, and clean minimalist engineering. Rejects all forms of generic AI slop.

## Core Rules:
1. **Reject generic aesthetics:** No neon purple/cyan gradients, no gratuitous glassmorphism, no non-functional floating pills/badges.
2. **Reject architectural slop:** No one-caller abstractions, no ghost code, no `any`/`ts-ignore` escapes.
3. **Reject copy & comment slop:** No banned buzzwords (*delve, tapestry, landscape, seamlessly, di era digital yang serba cepat ini*), no line-by-line narrative comments.
4. **Performance & responsiveness first:** 60fps animations, zero mobile overflow (`overflow-x: hidden`), zero layout shift (CLS = 0).
